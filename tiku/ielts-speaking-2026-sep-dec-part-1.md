# 2026 年 9-12 月雅思口语 Part 1

来源：北京新东方雅思《2026 年 9-12 月雅思口语题目完整版》。

## 新题

### 1. Travelling

1. Do you prefer to sit by the window when travelling?
2. Did you have a long journey with your family as a child?

### 2. Rubbish and recycling

1. Did you recycle when you were a kid?
2. Is waste sorting common in your country?
3. How do you recycle things like paper and plastic?
4. What do you do when you see rubbish on the street?

### 3. Tiredness

1. What do you do when you feel tired?
2. When would you feel tired?
3. Do you often feel tired?

### 4. Shoes

1. Which do you prefer, fashionable shoes or comfortable shoes?
2. How much money do you usually spend on shoes?
3. Have you ever bought shoes online?
4. Do you like buying shoes? How often?

### 5. Politeness

1. On what occasions should we be polite?
2. What did you do to show your politeness as a child?
3. Do you think being polite is very important?
4. How did you learn to be polite as a child?

### 6. Fruit and vegetables

1. Do people in your country like planting vegetables?
2. Were there any kind of fruits and vegetables you disliked as a child?
3. What kind of fruits and vegetables do you dislike?
4. Where do you usually buy fruit and vegetables?
5. How often do you eat fruit and vegetables?

### 7. Advertisement

1. Do you often see advertisements when you are on your phone or computer?
2. What kind of advertising do you like?
3. Do you like advertisements?
4. Do you see a lot of advertising on trains or other transport?
5. Is there an advertisement that made an impression on you when you were a child?

### 8. Paper

1. Do you carry paper and pens with you when you go out?
2. Do people still keep handwritten letters today?
3. Do you still write physical letters?
4. What did you like to do with paper as a child?
5. Have you made any crafts with paper?

### 9. Secondary school

1. Is there anything you miss about your secondary school?
2. What was your favourite subject at secondary school?
3. Were there any subjects that you found difficult at secondary school?
4. Do you remember your first day at secondary school?

### 10. Name

1. Do you often forget people's names?
2. How do you remember people's names?
3. Is it easy for you to remember people's names?
4. How do you feel when people can't remember your name?

## 保留题

### 1. Music

1. Do you prefer to sad or happy music?
2. Does happy music make you feel more excited?
3. Have you taken any music classes?
4. Do you listen to music while doing other things?

### 2. Teachers

1. Do you have a favorite teacher?
2. Do you want to be a teacher in the future?
3. Do you have a teacher from your past that you still remember?
4. Are you still in touch with your primary school teachers?
5. In what way has your favourite teacher helped you?
6. Do you like your primary school teachers more than your high school teachers?

### 3. Social media

1. Have you ever posted anything on social media?
2. When did you start using social media?
3. Do you think you spend too much time on social media?
4. Do your friends use social media?
5. What do people often do on social media?

### 4. Tidiness

1. Do you like to keep things tidy?
2. Did you keep your room tidy as a child?
3. How do you keep your work or study space tidy?
4. Do you think that it is necessary to be tidy?

### 5. Websites

1. What kinds of websites do you often visit?
2. What is your favourite website?
3. Are there any changes to the websites you often visit?
4. What kinds of websites are popular in your country?
5. Do you prefer getting information from websites or books?
6. Would you like to have your own website?
7. What have you learned from websites that help with your life or studies?

### 6. Watch

1. Do you wear a watch?
2. Have you ever got a watch as a gift?
3. Why do some people wear expensive watches?
4. Do you think it is important to wear a watch? Why?

### 7. Shopping

1. Do you like shopping?
2. How often do you go shopping?
3. Do you prefer online shopping or in-store shopping?
4. Have you ever returned anything you bought online?

### 8. Cars

1. Did you enjoy traveling by car when you were a kid?
2. What types of cars do you like?
3. Do you prefer to be a driver or a passenger?
4. What do you usually do when there is a traffic jam?
5. Do you think car colours are important?
6. Will you buy an expensive car in the future?

### 9. Public gardens and parks

1. Did you like going to parks as a child?
2. Do you still like going to parks now?
3. Would you like to see more parks in your city?
4. Are there any parks you want to go to in the future?
5. Would you prefer to play in a personal garden or public garden?
6. How are the parks today different from those you visited as a kid?
7. What do you like to do when visiting a park?

### 10. Science

1. Do you like science?
2. When did you start to learn about science?
3. Which science subject is interesting to you?
4. What kinds of interesting things have you done with science?
5. Do you like watching science TV programs?
6. Do Chinese people often visit science museums?

### 11. Mirrors

1. Have you ever bought mirrors?
2. Do you usually take a mirror with you?
3. Would you use mirrors to decorate your room?
4. Do you like looking at yourself in the mirror? How often?

### 12. Outer space and stars

1. Have you ever learnt about outer space and stars?
2. Do you like science fiction movies? Why?
3. Do you want to know more about outer space?
4. Do you want to go into outer space in the future?

### 13. Singing

1. Do you like singing? Why?
2. Have you ever learnt how to sing?
3. Who do you want to sing for?
4. Do you think singing can bring happiness to people?
5. Do you like listening to others singing?
6. Have you ever taken a singing class?

### 14. Clothing

1. What kind of clothes do you like to wear?
2. Do you prefer to wear comfortable and casual clothes or smart clothes?
3. Do you like wearing T-shirts?
4. Do you spend a lot of time choosing clothes?
5. Do you wear different styles of clothes on weekdays and weekends?
6. What colour clothes do you like?

### 15. Jokes & Comedies

1. Are you good at telling jokes?
2. Do your friends like to tell jokes?
3. Do you like to watch comedies?
4. Have you ever watched a live show?
5. Are comedy shows popular in your country?

### 16. Headphones

1. Do you use headphones?
2. What type of headphones do you use?
3. When would you use headphones?
4. In what conditions would you not use headphones?
5. Is wearing headphones comfortable?

## 万年老题

### 1. Work or studies

#### 学生版

1. What subjects are you studying?
2. Do you like your subject?
3. Why did you choose to study that subject?
4. Do you think that your subject is popular in your country?
5. Do you have any plans for your studies in the next five years?
6. What are the benefits of being your age?
7. Do you want to change your major?
8. Do you prefer to study in the mornings or in the afternoons?
9. How much time do you spend on your studies each week?
10. Are you looking forward to working?
11. What technology do you use when you study?
12. What changes would you like to see in your school?

#### 工作版

1. What work do you do?
2. Why did you choose to do that type of work?
3. Do you like your job?
4. What requirements did you need to meet to get your current job?
5. Do you have any plans for your work in the next five years?
6. What do you think is the most important at the moment?
7. Do you want to change to another job?
8. Do you miss being a student?
9. What technology do you use at work?
10. Who helps you the most? And how?

### 2. Home / accommodation

1. What kind of house or apartment do you want to live in in the future?
2. Are the transport facilities to your home very good?
3. Do you prefer living in a house or an apartment?
4. Please describe the room you live in.
5. What part of your home do you like the most?
6. How long have you lived there?
7. Do you plan to live there for a long time?
8. What’s the difference between where you are living now and where you have lived in the past?
9. Can you describe the place where you live?
10. What room does your family spend most of the time in?
11. What's your favorite room in your apartment or house?
12. What makes you feel pleasant in your home?
13. Do you think it is important to live in a comfortable environment?
14. Do you live in an apartment or a house?
15. Who do you live with?
16. What do you usually do in your apartment?
17. What kinds of accommodation do you live in?

### 3. Hometown

1. Where is your hometown?
2. Is that a big city or a small place?
3. Please describe your hometown a little.
4. How long have you been living there?
5. Do you think you will continue living there for a long time?
6. Do you like your hometown?
7. Do you like living there?
8. What do you like (most) about your hometown?
9. Is there anything you dislike about it?
10. What's your hometown famous for?
11. Did you learn about the history of your hometown at school?
12. Are there many young people in your hometown?
13. Is your hometown a good place for young people to pursue their careers?
14. Have you learned anything about the history of your hometown?
15. Did you learn about the culture of your hometown in your childhood?

### 4. The area you live in

1. Do you like the area that you live in?
2. Where do you like to go in that area?
3. Do you know any famous people in your area?
4. What are some changes in the area recently?
5. Do you know any of your neighbors?
6. Are the people in your neighborhood nice and friendly?
7. Do you live in a noisy or a quiet area?

### 5. The city you live in

1. What city do you live in?
2. Do you like this city? Why?
3. How long have you lived in this city?
4. Are there big changes in this city?
5. Is this city your permanent residence?
6. Are there people of different ages living in this city?
7. Are the people friendly in the city?
8. Is the city friendly to children and old people?
9. Do you often see your neighbors?
10. What's the weather like where you live?
11. Would you recommend your city to others?
